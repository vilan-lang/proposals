# E349 — A call inside a written paren group (`let v = (f(..));`) is measured without the group's `)` and the `;`: lines of 101 and 102 columns stay as written, 104 breaks

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-10-26 (fmt-55's find, Order 55)
- kind: fmt edge
- area: Formatter

The `LiftGroup` arm hands the split to its inside, but the call's own measure ends at its `)` and the terminator state belongs to the group's end, not the call's, so the group's closing paren and the `;` are two columns the call never counts (the shape E329 closed for an unwrapped call, E338 for a hole). Same fix family as E337: shift `terminator_end` to the inner expression's end and add the `)` to `terminator_columns` while printing a group at the value's end. Sizing XS.
