# A59 — `std::dom` cannot MEASURE, OBSERVE, or un-set: kolt's overlay.vl carries twenty externs under a hand-written "DOM bindings std::dom lacks yet" banner (`getBoundingClientRect`, `contains`, `offsetWidth/Height`, `isConnected`, capture-phase listeners, `ResizeObserver`) and views.vl adds `removeAttribute` — the other half of `set_attribute`, without which `toggle_attr` cannot be written

- status: OPEN — the owner's kolt TODO/FIXME sweep (2026-09-07)
- kind: feature
- area: Reactive core & UI

WHAT STD HAS TODAY: vilan/std/src/browser/dom.vl — `set_text` (:47), `set_class` (:51), `set_attribute`
(:55-56), `set_style_property` (:61-62), `append`/`remove`/`clear` (:65-79), `set_hidden` (:82-83), `focus`
(:89-90), `value`/`set_value` (:94-99), `on`/`on_event`/`off_event` and the `[must_use]` `listen` wrapper
(:102-132) on Element, the same listener trio plus `listen` (:154-171) on Window. Nothing measures, nothing
observes, nothing removes an attribute, and both `listen` wrappers hard-code the BUBBLE phase.

Each binding has a named customer in kolt's own anchored-overlay path: it measures the anchor
(`bounding_rect`) and itself (`offset_width`/`offset_height`); it waits on `is_connected` before
positioning, because a detached element measures 0x0; it asks `contains` whether an outside click should
dismiss it; it listens for `scroll` in the CAPTURE phase because, in the owner's own comment, that is "the
only way to hear `scroll` from every scrolling ancestor at once, which is what keeps an anchored menu on its
element while the page scrolls under it"; and it re-positions on `ResizeObserver`. views.vl's
`remove_attribute` is the missing half of `set_attribute` — its `toggle_attr` (views.vl:384-396) sets the
attribute to `""` when true and must delete it when false, which today's surface cannot express.

PROPOSED, in rising order of how much design each needs:
1. `Element::remove_attribute` beside `set_attribute`. An omission, not a design question.
2. `listen_capture` beside `listen` on Element and Window, same `[must_use]` `Subscription` contract.
3. A measurement surface: `DomRect` plus `Element::bounding_rect`, `offset_width`, `offset_height`,
   `is_connected`, `contains`.
4. Resize observation. Prefer the wrapper kolt built over the raw class — `observe_resize(owner, target,
   on_resize)` (overlay.vl:128) fires once on first render and disconnects when the owner disposes, which is
   the shape an app wants; the bare `ResizeObserver` is a `retains` callback that leaks unless every caller
   remembers to disconnect it.
