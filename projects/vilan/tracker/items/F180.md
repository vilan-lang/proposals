# F180 — `kit::text_area`: one key costs 42 ms at 1,000 lines and 0.49 s at 10,000 (the whole text is one paragraph, reshaped twice per change)

- status: open
- kind: perf (native UI; text editing)
- area: vilan/std/src/ui/kit/text_areas.vl, crates/vilan-rt-gfx/src/text/, crates/vilan-rt-gfx/src/ui/mod.rs

First layout 43 ms / 583 ms (1,000 / 10,000 lines); one key 42 ms / 487 ms; a caret move 14 us / 158 us. The two reshapes per key are at the widest width and at the narrowest (min-content): the stack inside the area is a grid whose column sizing asks for the narrowest width. STEP 1 (small): stop the grid asking for min-content on an edited text (halves it). STEP 2 (the real fix): one text element per paragraph, so a key reshapes one paragraph; the caret, selection, vertical moves and the accessibility runs then cross elements. Also seen, not investigated: Page Down clamps the scroll offset to 58 where the caret's reveal clamps to 57.5 (existing scroll machinery). DECIDED IN LANE, owner may overrule: an index at a soft wrap is on the LOWER line (no affinity bit; End lands before the last grapheme of a wrapped line); triple click selects the paragraph; Enter types a newline and Ctrl+Enter submits; undo keeps 100 changes, typing coalesces until the caret moves, a write from outside clears the history; a selected line break is a quarter of the line height wide; a long word breaks only in a multi-line text. Not built: clipboard keys (F173), IME (a preedit run at the caret the value does not hold), blink and auto-scroll (F176).

STEP 1 TRIED 2026-11-13 (lane kit-wire), NOT DONE: taffy's grid asks every item in an intrinsic track for its min-content contribution unconditionally (`track_sizing.rs`), so `min_width = 0` on the edited text did not remove the width-0 shaping (counted: still 2 shapings a key). Real options: answer min-content from the kept grapheme geometry without reshaping; give the area's stack a non-intrinsic track; or step 2 (one element per paragraph).
