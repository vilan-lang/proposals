# N41 — the ledger gate's coverage check has a catch-all row and a helper blind spot

- status: OPEN — b179 lane's finds (2026-09-01); extends N36's shipped gate
- kind: process
- area: Hygiene & rot
- source: lane b179

Two holes in the day-old gate, found by its first customer:
(1) Row 291's key is `{headline}{subject}: {}` — all slots — and
`key_describes` lets a leading slot skip, so the key matches ANY
message containing `": "`. Six of 250 enumerated sites are held only
by row 291, five accidentally (transformer.rs:2487,
platform_color.rs:757, analyzer.rs:4458/12199/32082). Check (3)
needs the exclusion `KEYS_WITHOUT_A_FRAGMENT` already gives check (2).
(2) A message built into a `let msg` HELPER is skipped by
`anchored_messages` — documented, but it means the `+` ladder's six
messages (the family the appendix documents most heavily, B179's new
sentence included) have no enumeration coverage. Extend the anchor
walk one assignment upstream, or row them by hand with a stated
exemption.
