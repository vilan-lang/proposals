# M180 — `Expect: 100-continue` answered when the handler READS, not at the head - node sends `100 Continue` before the handler runs, so a client that asks (curl does for bodies over 1 MiB) uploads a body the handler is about to refuse from the head

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-10-26 (http-55's find, Order 55)
- kind: feature (std::http; S) - M164's lazy body, the last mile
- area: Standard library

With no `checkContinue` listener node writes `100 Continue` as soon as the head arrives; after M164 the handler may answer `401` without reading, but the client has already been told to send. Natively no `100` is ever sent (curl waits its 1 s and sends anyway). Rec: `server.on("checkContinue", handler)` on node with `__http_read_body` calling `response.writeContinue()` on the first read; natively the same at `read_body`'s first poll when the head carries `Expect: 100-continue`. Pins: a client sending `Expect: 100-continue` and a 2 MiB declared body to a `401`-from-head route receives the `401` and no `100`; to a reading route the `100` then the body.
