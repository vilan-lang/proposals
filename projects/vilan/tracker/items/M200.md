# M200 — `Store<List<T: Keyed>>::by_key` scans the list on EVERY read and write through the handle (`keyed_element` / `keyed_write`, store.vl:884-900): a field patch costs O(list length), and one turn patching every row of a 32,000-row list takes 5.3 s

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-11-05 (the integrator's find, measuring the reactive lens lab against Store)
- kind: performance (std::reactive::store; keyed lists)
- area: Standard library / reactive

by_key's doc says 'the first element whose key is `key`' and both closures it hands `descend` walk the list for the hash. Rec: keep a key -> position index on the sequence node, maintained by the node's own splices (it already records a SeqOp per handle write) and rebuilt on a whole write; `keyed_element` / `keyed_write` consult it and fall back to the scan when it is absent. Pin: the wide turn at 32,000 rows under a ceiling that a scan cannot meet (a count of element visits is steadier than a clock); a differential against the scan for reads after splices, moves and whole writes.
