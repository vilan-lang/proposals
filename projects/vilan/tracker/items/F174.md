# F174 — `std::ui::kit::virtual_list`: what the first version leaves out (estimated row extents, its own padding, positions past 4 million dp, a focused row scrolled away)

- status: open
- kind: feature gap (native UI; kit)
- area: vilan/std/src/ui/kit/lists.vl, crates/vilan-rt-gfx/src/ui

(1) ESTIMATED EXTENTS: rows of differing heights need a resize notice per row (the host's `on_resize` is in), a prefix-sum tree over n heights to map an offset to an index, and scroll anchoring when a measured height differs from its estimate; vilan work only, the lane's guess is a day. (2) The list's own padding is not subtracted from the viewport height. (3) Row positions are `f32`: beyond about 4 million dp (125,000 rows of 32 dp) a position loses part of a pixel; positions relative to the window's first row would remove the limit. (4) A focused row the wheel scrolls out of the window is removed and loses the focus; decide whether the focused row is kept built (the web's virtual lists differ here). (5) A deferred reveal does not update the hover until the next pointer move. DECIDED IN LANE, owner may overrule: the extent is a required argument; overscan defaults to 2; Tab builds one row ahead before the focus moves and leaves the list at its real ends; an insert above the window keeps the offset; `reveal` moves as little as needed; the list sets `role(List)` and leaves row roles to the app; `on_resize` and `impl Element with Attributes` are public; the resize-and-layout loop is capped at 8 passes.
