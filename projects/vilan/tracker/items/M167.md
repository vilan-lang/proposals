# M167 — `std::web::router` has no history state: `navigate` and `navigate_replace` always write `""`, and an app can't read `history.state`

- status: OPEN — filed 2026-10-21 (kolt's panel layout: back and forward must know which panel a history entry belongs to)
- kind: API gap (std::web::router; S)
- area: std

`router.vl` calls `history.pushState("", "", path)` and `history.replaceState("", "", path)`. Its `history_push` and `history_replace` externs aren't exported, and nothing reads `history.state`. So an app that needs to know *what* a history entry was, and not just its path, has to:

1. write its own `[extern]` for `history.state` and for `replaceState` with a state;
2. re-mark every entry right after `navigate` pushed it (`replaceState` over the empty state); and
3. tell a back/forward pop apart from a `navigate` by comparing marks inside a `current_path()` effect, since `popstate` doesn't reach the app as an event with the entry's state.

Kolt does all three. Back and forward have to swap the panel that showed a page, not whichever panel has focus, so each entry records which panel showed it.

Recommendation: `navigate_with(path, state: str)` / `navigate_replace_with(path, state)`, a `history_state(): str`, and either a `popped()` signal or a `current_entry(): SignalCell<(str, str)>` (path, state) that tells pops apart from pushes.
