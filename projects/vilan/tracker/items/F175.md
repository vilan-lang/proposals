# F175 — Paths and icons for `std::gfx` and `std::ui` (native-ui.md P5): the paper `gfx-path.md`, thirteen decisions for the owner

- status: open
- kind: feature (native UI; paths and icons) — RULED 2026-11-12, R1-R13
- area: crates/vilan-rt-gfx (atlas mask pages), vilan/std/src/gfx.vl, vilan/std/src/ui.vl, an icon package outside std

RECOMMENDATION: draw a path as a CPU coverage mask made by `zeno` (already in the tree through `swash`), stored in the glyph atlas's mask pages and drawn as a tinted quad, the way a glyph is: no new package, shader or pipeline. Measured on Mesa software Vulkan (no real GPU): coverage error at 16 dp, mean (worst pixel): zeno mask 0.022 (0.18), tiny-skia mask 0.023 (0.12), lyon with 4x MSAA 0.052 (0.31), no anti-aliasing 0.161 (0.78); 240 cached icons a frame cost 83 us of CPU against 72 us for 240 rounded rectangles and 250 us as lyon triangles at 4x MSAA; a new 24 dp icon is 2 to 48 us to parse and rasterise. `lyon` stays the fallback for large paths that change every frame (a 200-point chart line: zeno 93 us, lyon 17 us); `vello` stays rejected (compute shaders the WebGL2 limits refuse). Surface: `gfx::Path` from `Path::from_svg` or a builder, `Stroke`, `PathPaint`, `Frame::path(path, at, scale, paint, color)`; an `icon` element in `std::ui` coloured by the inherited text colour; icon sets as generated packages outside std, Lucide first. The decisions Q1-Q13 are the paper's section 9, each with a recommended answer. Slices after the ruling: S1 the primitive in the gfx crate, S2 the Vilan surface, S3 the `icon` element, S4 a Lucide package. Least sure of: thin strokes blended in linear light read lighter than a browser draws them (shared with text's open gamma question); zeno's stroker over a whole icon set (seven icons were drawn, typed from memory of Lucide's shapes).

RULED 2026-11-12: the owner accepted all thirteen recommendations ("Accept all recs"); the paper's section 9 carries them as R1-R13. BUILD ORDER: S1 the primitive in `vilan-rt-gfx` and S2 the `std::gfx` surface (lane path-s1, started 2026-11-12 off next 2f7aa355); then S3 the `icon` element in `std::ui`; then S4 a generator and a Lucide package outside std, with the full-set sweep against a reference rasteriser. The spike stays on branch `path-58` @05259d63 (R13).

S4 LANDED 2026-11-13 (lane lucide-s4 a7ea9738, merged next 93afb97f, pushed; full suite 11,640 green): `vilan/packages/lucide`, a `[library]` generated by `scripts/lucide-native.mjs` from lucide-static 1.64.0 (ISC; tarball integrity in the header): 1,999 icons as `export fun name(): Icon`, plus `icons.tsv` for the Rust sweep; taken by a path dependency. THE SWEEP (`crates/vilan-rt-gfx/src/path/lucide_sweep.rs`, 2.5 s in debug, every icon at 16, 24 and 48 dp against `tiny-skia =0.11.4` as a dev-dependency adding no package): every icon parses and passes (mean absolute coverage difference: average 0.051 / 0.027 / 0.014, worst pixel 0.62 / 0.53 / 0.69). It found two defects in how strokes are outlined, both FIXED in `stroke_outline` with pins seen red: a contour shorter than zeno's 0.01 merge distance drew nothing (15 icons lost a dot, `signal-zero` everything), and a round join turning back 180 degrees came out flat (18 icons had a notch). MITER VERDICT: Lucide is round joins and caps throughout, so R5's gap does not touch it; drawn with miter joins as an experiment, 56 icons went up to a whole pixel off, so a set that uses miter joins needs a fix (F183). The `tiny-skia` fallback is NOT needed for this set. What is left is overlap darkening at stroke edges: F183 (the owner's eye). Distribution of packages outside a clone, the check cost of a declared dependency and two tool gaps: F184. ALL FOUR SLICES OF THE PAPER ARE BUILT (R1-R13).
