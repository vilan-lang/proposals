# A32 — custom signals with compatibility: `Source` is the trait, and the question is its reach

- status: OPEN — owner-proposed 2026-08-29 ("should Signal become a
  trait?"); reframed on the finding that the trait already exists
- kind: design
- area: Reactive core & UI
- source: the owner's item, 2026-08-29; kolt's StorageSignal the live exhibit

The ask: custom signal logic (a persisted signal, a debounced one, a
computed view) implementable while staying compatible with everything that
consumes signals. The finding: **std already has the readable trait** —
`Source<T>` (`get`/`sub`/`effect`-default, reactive.vl ~429), charter
verbatim "code that only observes can't tell a local signal from a remote
one" — and kolt's `StorageSignal<T>` implements it TODAY. What remains is
design, not invention:

- **Reach** — the consumers are the gap, not the trait (A33's audit: the
  UI bindings take concrete `Signal`).
- **Combinators** — `map`/`combine`/`flatten` live on `Signal`;
  `RemoteSource::map` exists separately. Generic combinators over any
  `Source` would unify the three worlds (and meet B157's `MaybeSignal`
  vocabulary — one family of bounds, not three).
- **The write side** — is a `Sink<T>`/writable trait wanted (`set`/
  `set_with`), or does writing stay concrete? kolt's StorageSignal
  duplicates the write surface by hand today. Bindings that WRITE BACK
  (`bind_value`, `bind_draft`) are the consumers that would bound on it.
- **The owner's direction, 2026-08-29 evening (superseding the lean
  above): `Signal` DOES become a trait** — the writable equivalent of
  `Source` (set/set_with/notify over Source's get/sub/effect). The
  motivating case, verbatim shape: a component takes `Signal<u32>`; the
  user passes an impl whose SET clamps (write 1000, store 800) via a
  transformer callback — satisfying the component's bound with custom
  write logic. `Signal::new(value)` still exists and returns the
  canonical cell impl, so the spelling stays the same in practice.
- **The constructor constraint the paper must solve:** vilan has no
  trait-associated statics (the i32::max_value stopgap records exactly
  this gap), so if `Signal` names the TRAIT, `Signal::new` cannot hang
  on it as shipped. Routes: (a) trait statics as a feature (the general
  fix, and i32::MAX's own future); (b) the cell keeps the name `Signal`
  as a struct and the trait takes another name with bounds still reading
  well — REJECTED by the ask as stated (the component's bound is spelled
  `Signal<u32>`); (c) a free constructor (`signal(v)`) — changes the
  spelling; (d) namespace unification (an inherent `impl` on a trait
  name). The paper weighs these; (a) serves two standing items at once.
- **Clamp semantics to state:** after `set(1000)`, `get` reads 800 —
  observers and the setter's caller see the transformed value; equality/
  no-op-notify rides the impl. Interactions: B157's `MaybeSignal` bounds
  and B158's blanket impls compose with trait-Signal (an
  `impl S with MaybeSignal<T>` over any `S: Signal<T>` is the blanket
  family's next test case).
