# N201 — gfx goldens across Mesa versions: CI's llvmpipe (Mesa 25.2.8) put one pixel of `paths-125` two off a golden made on this box's Mesa 25.0.7

- status: open
- kind: harness (golden determinism)
- area: crates/vilan-rt-gfx/tests/support/mod.rs (`assert_golden_allowing`, `differences`, `within`), crates/vilan-rt-gfx/tests/paths.rs (`EDGE_STRAGGLERS`)

The path sheets hold some 30,000 edge pixels, each a coverage blended over the background and then encoded to sRGB; two versions of one software rasteriser round the blend's last bit differently, and the encode stretches that bit to two at a dark value. The fix: `assert_golden_allowing(.., stragglers)` passes when at most `stragglers` channel values are past the tolerance and none is more than one further; the path sheets allow 12 (four pixels' worth); every other golden keeps the plain tolerance of 1. The rule is pinned (`a_few_edge_values_one_further_off_pass_and_nothing_else_does`, red with the bound on the largest difference removed). STILL OPEN: (1) the same can happen to any golden with many blended edges (text sheets, the UI screens) on the next Mesa bump of the runner image or of this box; decide then whether the allowance becomes the general rule. (2) The failure image is written under the crate's target directory on the runner and is not uploaded: add an upload-artifact step for `crates/vilan-rt-gfx/target/golden-failures/` and the vilan-cli equivalents on failure, so a mismatch can be LOOKED at instead of reasoned about. (3) This box cannot reproduce CI's Mesa; a container with the runner's Mesa would let goldens be made on what CI runs.
