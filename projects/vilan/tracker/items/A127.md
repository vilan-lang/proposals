A second constructor rather than a field on `HttpTransport` (a field would break existing struct literals)

- status: OPEN — filed 2026-09-22 (Order 40 sweep)
- kind: feature (std)
- area: Standard library

Sizing S; the headers are sent on every POST of that client; a pin over the S4 table's 401 row turning 200 with the header.
