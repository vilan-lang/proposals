# F62 — native — reading or writing a FIELD through a `Shared` VIEW (`s.write().n`, `s.write().n = x`) is refused by name; A142's `OwnerCell` and S6's `TrackRuns` are written WHOLE to avoid it, and `a.write().n = a.write().n + 1` (vilan/test/shared.vl) needs a temporary (the right side's borrow would stay live across the left's)

- status: OPEN — filed 2026-09-30 (Order 44, tracking-44's find 2 — the third lane this order to design around it)
- kind: bug (native — refusal by name; the F1 S1b gap)
- area: Native targets & rendering

Emit a field read/write through a view as a scoped borrow (read: `{ let g = s.borrow(); g.n.clone() }`; write: `s.borrow_mut().n = x`), and hoist the right-hand side into a temporary when it reads the same cell. Pins: read, write, the read-modify-write line, both backends. Then `OwnerCell`/`TrackRuns` may go back to field writes (reactive's call). Sizing S–M; native, Order 45.
