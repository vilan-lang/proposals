E275 ruled that a written `Debug` impl decides what `dbg` prints; the S7 sidecar records the printer's STRUCTURAL layout only, because naming the impl would mean asking the transformer for the impl's emitted function, and the sidecar may not cause emission (the script must stay byte-identical with and without it).
