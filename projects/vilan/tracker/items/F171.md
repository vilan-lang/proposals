# F171 — gfx host on X11: give the GPU surface its own X connection, so no driver thread reads winit's (the root-cause fix behind F169's wake schedule)

- status: open
- kind: design question (native runtime; needs `unsafe`) — OWNER RULING
- area: crates/vilan-rt-gfx/src/host (surface creation), wgpu's raw-handle surface target

winit and wgpu share one X connection today because the surface is made from the window. A second connection opened for the surface alone would keep Mesa's swapchain thread off winit's socket, and the wake schedule could go. It needs raw window and display handles (`unsafe`), which `vilan-rt-gfx` forbids by lint. QUESTION for the owner: allow one audited `unsafe` block in the gfx crate for this, or keep the schedule. Integrator's recommendation: keep the schedule until a real-GPU run shows a loss it does not cover; the schedule is cheap, proven by 200 runs, and platform-neutral in its failure mode. Also unknown: NVIDIA's driver; Mesa hardware drivers likely share the defect (Present events read on their swapchain threads) and are covered by the schedule.
