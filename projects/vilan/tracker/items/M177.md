# M177 — `multipart/form-data` parsed on the body stream - parts (headers, name, filename) read incrementally, each part's bytes a stream of their own

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-10-26 (http-55's follow-up, Order 55; R-g)
- kind: feature (std::http; M) - after M176
- area: Standard library

Kolt and every browser-form upload send `multipart/form-data`; today a handler gets the whole body and parses it by hand (kolt sidesteps it by POSTing the file raw with `X-Kolt-Filename`). After M176's reader: a `Multipart` over `BodyReader` (boundary from `Content-Type`, RFC 7578), `next_part(): Option<Part>` with `name`, `filename`, `content_type` and a part `Reader`, the cap the body's. The rpc POST leg keeps refusing multipart (its CSRF gate, docs/std/rpc.md). Pins: a two-part form with a 10 MiB file part at a few MiB of RSS; a boundary split across chunk edges; a malformed part refused 400.
