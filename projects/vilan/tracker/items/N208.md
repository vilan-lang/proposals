# N208 — The perf harness measures a frozen pre-reorganisation Kolt, and its Kolt check row reads about 27% lower at seals run across a breaking change than at ordinary seals on the same source

- status: open
- kind: harness (perf exhibit; two defects)
- area: scripts/perf_gate.py (the untracked-input list near line 456, the `--tip-kolt` path), scripts/lsp-latency.py (`copy_untracked_inputs`, the scenario files), proposals/scripts/integration/seal.sh

(1) LAYOUT. Kolt was reorganised on 2026-11-09 (`src/server`, `src/client`, `src/shared`, library packages under `lib/`, generated inputs at `lib/lucide/src/icons` and `lib/search-dict/data`). The harness carries the OLD untracked inputs (`src/lucide`, `src/search-dict`, `search-dict`) and its E121 scenarios edit files by their old paths (`shared.vl`, `model.vl`, the css file). So every seal since measures a copy of Kolt from before the reorganisation with patches committed on top, not the program the owner works on, which has grown since. Migrate the input list and the scenario table to the new layout, re-anchor the E121 series as N186 did, and state the new baseline. (2) MODE. The same Kolt source reads about 33.7 G instructions for `vilan check` at a seal run with a separate prepared tip tree (v0.54.0, v0.56.0: both sides, base and tip) and about 46.1 G at a same-source seal (v0.55.0 on c7454052, v0.56.1 on 76067e7a: both sides), under compilers that differ by under 1%. The exhibit trees are byte-identical outside `.git` (checked with `diff -rq` on 2026-11-17: order58/kolt, order58b/kolt and order58/kolt-tip differ only by the db patch). Peak RSS follows the same split (510 to 530 MB against 589 to 594 MB). Each seal's RATIO stands, since both of its sides are measured one way, but the absolute rows in `perf/report-v*.md` are not comparable across the two kinds of seal, and one of the two modes is measuring less than the other believes. Find which (a second entry or package checked in one mode and not the other is the first guess), make the two modes measure the same thing, and say in the report which kind of seal a row came from.
