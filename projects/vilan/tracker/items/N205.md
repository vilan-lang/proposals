# N205 — `bump-version.sh` does not reach the two crates outside the workspace: `crates/vilan-rt-gfx` and `crates/vilan-rt-webview` keep the old version in their own `Cargo.lock` and in their notices' `Covers:` line, and CI's `gfx` and `webview` legs would refuse the release commit

- status: open
- kind: release tooling (the cut; first release with these crates)
- area: scripts/bump-version.sh, scripts/cut-release.sh (`release_files`), crates/vilan-cli/tests/release_scripts.rs

The bump rewrites every `crates/*/Cargo.toml` and runs `cargo update --workspace`, which refreshes the ROOT lockfile only. The two excluded crates have their own lockfiles holding `vilan-rt`, `vilan-rt-poll` and themselves by path, and `ci-local.sh`'s legs run `cargo fetch --locked` and byte-compare the notices, whose `Covers:` line names those three versions. Do NOT fix it with `cargo update -p ..` inside the crate: on 2026-11-16 that also re-resolved four `windows-sys 0.61.2` edges to `0.52.0` in the gfx lockfile. Edit the three `version = ` lines under their `name = ` lines, then `cargo fetch --locked` to prove the lock stands, then regenerate the notices. `release_files` must list the four files so a `--commit` cut stages them, and `release_scripts.rs` should hold the set to the root manifest's `exclude` list the way it holds the members.
