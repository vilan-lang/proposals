# B232 — a leftover `Constraint::MethodCall` after the fixpoint produces no residual diagnostic of its own; B229's clean-program guard exists only because of it

- status: OPEN — lane context-27's find (2026-09-04, Order 27)
- kind: bug
- area: Type system & the type solver

The post-solve residual sweep reports StructInitializer/FieldAccessor/Variable/CallSubject leftovers only — a stalled method call is spoken about only by whatever error stalled it. B229 therefore gates its stand-down on `program.diagnostics` being non-empty, or a stalled `run` on a clean program would turn a fence into a silent miscompile. Give `MethodCall` a residual ("this call could not be resolved: …" naming the unresolved operand), then drop the guard. Pins: a clean program with a stalled method call reports once; B229's suite unchanged.
