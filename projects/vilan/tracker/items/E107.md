# E107 — no completion at a builder chain's dot on its own line

- status: OPEN — the owner's report, 2026-08-29 evening
- kind: bug
- area: LSP & tooling
- source: the owner's kolt interact.vl session

Inside a parenthesized builder chain, a `.` on its own line between two
existing `.calls` offers nothing:
`InteractionHandler::new()\n.on_drag(...)\n.|\n.on_double_click(...)`.
The likely mechanism: recovery parses `.on_double_click` as the chain's
continuation, orphaning the bare dot, and the cursor context falls out of
member position. This is the completion-context CLASS (kolt.local 001's
family — one cursor context) wearing a new face: fix where the context is
computed, not the symptom; pin the mid-chain dot, the trailing dot, and the
dot-before-close-paren shapes.
