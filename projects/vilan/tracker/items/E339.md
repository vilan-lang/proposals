# E339 — After E329 a call that IS a statement's value counts the `;`, but a binary's right operand (`let v = 1 + f(..);`) and a chain link keep the documented rule that the `;` is the statement's: the same 100-column call splits as `let v = f(..);` and stays at 101 columns as `let v = 1 + f(..);`

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-10-16 (fmt-54's find, Order 54); a design question, owner rec KEEP
- kind: fmt layout question
- area: Formatter

A first attempt counted the `;` for a binary's right operand too (print_split_right measured with the terminator); `binary_operand_layout::a_right_operand_at_the_budget_stays_inline_and_one_column_over_splits` documents the opposite as deliberate ('the `;` the statement glues after the operand is the statement's, not the operand's ... the same rule a chain link's line follows'), so E329 was kept to the Call arm and the pin untouched. The edge: `let v = f(..);` 100 columns before the `;` splits; `let v = 1 + f(..);` 100 columns before the `;` stays one 101-column line (the split arms and then declines). Question for the owner: keep (the pin's rule), or make the right operand count the `;` as the call now does (one `terminator_after` call in print_split_right plus re-pinning that test)? Rec: keep for now; two statement shapes differ by one column in a case that does not occur in the estate.
