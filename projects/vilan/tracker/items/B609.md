# B609 — A subtrait impl over a STRUCTURAL subject (a tuple or a fixed array) cannot reach its supertrait's impl through an element's sub-bound — `impl (type A: Louder, type B: Louder) with Loud {}` (with `trait Loud with Shown`, `trait Louder with Display` and `impl (type A: Display, type B: Display) with Shown`) is refused "'(A, B)' does not implement trait 'Loud': missing 'shown'"; the nominal twin (`impl Box2<type T: Eq> with Eq {}` over `impl Box2<type T: PartialEq> with PartialEq`) compiles

- status: OPEN — filed 2026-10-10 (lang-50's find, Order 50)
- kind: bug (checker; S)
- area: Traits & impls

Pre-existing on v0.47.0 for tuples; array-lengths S5 met it as `impl [type T: Eq; _] with Eq {}` beside std's new `impl [type T: PartialEq; const N] with PartialEq`, so arrays (and tuples) ship without `Eq`. The conformance walk that looks for the supertrait member on the subtrait impl's subject finds the supertrait impl for a nominal subject whose binder's bound implies the supertrait impl's element bound (Eq => PartialEq), and does not for a tuple/array subject. Expected: compiles, and `fun needs<T: Eq>(v: T)` accepts a `[i32; 2]` once std writes the impl. Sizing S; the supertrait-member search in conformance.
