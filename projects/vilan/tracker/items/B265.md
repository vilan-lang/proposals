- status: OPEN — lane perf-29's find (2026-09-06, Order 29)
- kind: bug

Bind intrinsics to `external fun` declarations only (a bodied declaration of the same name keeps its body), or refuse a bodied declaration of an intrinsic name with a message; pin both.
