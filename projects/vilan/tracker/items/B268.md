# B268 — a `Signal<View>` (or `SignalCell<View>`) in CHILD position stringifies instead of rendering and re-rendering (kolt views.vl:543's HACK: `.swap(signal, |x| view)` stands in for `{signal}`); with A46's fragment the question is the same — WHAT MAY BE A CHILD (str, View, List<View>, a Signal of each), and a `View` struct cannot say it while a trait could

- status: OPEN — the owner's kolt find (2026-09-07); DESIGN with A46
- kind: bug
- area: Reactive core & UI

Today a child `{expr}` desugars to `.child(expr)` and a `Signal<str>` child binds text; a `Signal<View>` child reaches the string path and renders the View's runtime shape. Pin it red; then rule the child contract: a `trait Child` (or `Render`) implemented by `str`, `View`, `List<View>` (A46's fragment), `Signal<str>`, `Signal<View>` and `Signal<List<View>>` — the signal impls own their re-render (a `swap` under the row's owner) — and `.child` takes `impl Child`. Whether `View` itself becomes a trait is the larger question A46 carries; the child trait does not require it and can land first.
