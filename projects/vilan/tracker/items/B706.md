# B706 — A `let` view is live until the END of its scope, not its last use: `let s = map.slot(k); s.take(); map.insert(..)` is refused after `s`'s last use

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-11-04 (lane std-56's find)
- kind: bug (checker; view liveness)
- area: Checker / borrows

Rec: end a view binding's liveness at its last use (the LastUse record exists for this), so the M191 idiom composes with a following insert; pin both the accepted shape and the refusal when the view IS used after. Sizing S.
