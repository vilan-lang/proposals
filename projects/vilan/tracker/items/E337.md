# E337 — A call followed by `!`, `?` or a field access (`f(a, b, c)!;`, `f(..).field;`) never breaks its arguments, at any width: the statement hook arms the split but the postfix prints its subject without the permission

- status: OPEN - filed 2026-10-16 (fmt-54's find, Order 54)
- kind: fmt edge
- area: Formatter

Found probing E329 (the terminator width). `let value = plain_function_call_with_no_chain_at_all(argument_one, argument_two, <150 columns>)!;`, `.field;` and `?;` stay one line after `vilan fmt` at any length, while the same call with no postfix breaks one argument per line (E321) and `1 + f(..)` breaks too. `Node::TryAssert` / a non-call postfix print their subject through `print_operand` with no split permission, so the Call arm's `split != Split::Off` gate never opens. Likely the fix is to hand the permission to the subject of `!`/`?`/a field step when the postfix is the statement's value (the E329 `terminator_end` check then counts the `;` against the postfix's end, not the call's). The estate has no such line over the budget today (kolt, std, website, examples and the native tests unchanged). Sizing S.
