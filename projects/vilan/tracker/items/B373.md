- status: OPEN — filed 2026-09-21 (Order 39 sweep; std-39's find 2)
- kind: bug (std codec)
- area: std::json / wire

Repro: `i32::from_json("1.5")` answers `Ok(1.5)`; `u32::from_json("-1")` likely `Ok(-1)`. The typed READER checks whole-number / non-negative since 2321790e; the scalar impls parse and hand the number through. Fix: route the scalars through the same lane checks (or share the predicate); pins per scalar lane (fractional into integer, negative into unsigned, string into number).
