# A43 — `Source` has no lazy subscribe: land the `observe`-shaped member (`on_change`) that reactive-traits Q5 owes, rather than flipping `sub`/`effect`

- status: OPEN — the owner's ask while migrating kolt (2026-09-04); triage lane's survey at 289e2a2b
- kind: design
- area: Reactive core & UI

The owner: `sub` and `effect` are eager; often not desired. Confirmed:
`sub` = `observe(self, observer); observer(self.get())` (reactive.vl
~523); `effect` = `get_owner().take(self.sub(..))` (~462). The lazy
primitive EXISTS but is std-internal: `observe` (~547, doc'd "sub
WITHOUT the immediate first call"). Contract: docs/std/reactive.md ~128.

Census: `.sub(` kolt 3 / std 6 / examples 3 / test 7; `.effect(` kolt 6
/ std 16 / examples 1 / test 5. ZERO sites in any corpus guard against
the first call; the sites that RELY on it: kolt probe.vl ~33 (reads
immediately), views.vl ~484/514/522, input_system.vl ~29, overlay.vl
~564; std `flatten` (~724), rpc `expose` (~1172) and `lease` (~1497),
all three commented as relying on it; and ALL 14 browser/ui.vl bindings,
which are the initial paint. Solid's `createEffect` is the same
(immediate, then on change); its opt-out is `on(deps, fn, {defer:
true})` — vilan has no optional parameters or overloading, so the
opt-out must be a NAME. Only genuinely redundant eager site: `combine`
(~705) re-sets `derived` n times to its constructed value.

Recommendation: do not flip. Add `Source::on_change(observer):
Subscription` as a trait requirement (SignalCell's impl = today's
`observe`) plus an owner-registered `effect_on_change`. reactive-
traits.md Q5 (~860) asks for exactly this ("widening `map` needs an
observe-shaped member on Source") and it was left unbuilt when A32/A33
closed — `map` is still inherent on SignalCell (~632). One member pays
two debts; `combine` moves to it and drops the n redundant sets. Size S
(M with the `map` widening). Owner's call: if the owner still wants
eager-by-name instead (`sub_now`), the migration list above is the
cost. Related: reactive-traits Q5 and ~131–136; reactive-batching.md
~89 ("sub's immediate fire is unchanged"); A14; A32/A33 (closed); A44.
