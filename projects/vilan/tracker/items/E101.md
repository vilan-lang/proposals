# E101 — `str` has no `index_of`/`find`, and three diagnostics do not name their cause

- status: OPEN — the lucide evidence run's std-gap and papercut collection (Order 19)
- kind: feature
- area: LSP & tooling
- source: the lucide-evidence lane, writing a real generator in vilan, 2026-08-28

Two halves, one small lane. (1) The std gap: no `index_of`/`find` on
`str` (and no regex anywhere) — the generator parsed SVG by
`split("\"")` and `substring`, workable but the only tool there is; an
`index_of`/`find` pair is the smallest honest step (regex is its own
future question). (2) Three diagnostics that cost real time by not
naming their cause: `pub` (not a keyword) reports "expected `;` to end
this statement" at column 3; `let mut x` (the spelling is `mut x`)
misleads similarly; a literal `{` in an i-string (the escape is `\{`)
reports "found ')' expected an expression" — and a code GENERATOR writes
braces constantly. Each wants the did-you-mean note the tree's
diagnostic voice already uses elsewhere.
