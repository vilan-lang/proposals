# A33 — the Source-not-Signal audit: bindings demand a `Signal` they never write

- status: OPEN — owner-filed 2026-08-29; audit + mechanical widening
- kind: design
- area: Reactive core & UI
- source: the owner's item, 2026-08-29; kolt's StorageSignal cannot feed `bind_attr`

Every reactive binding in `browser/ui.vl` takes CONCRETE `Signal` —
`bind_text`, `bind_class`, `bind_styled`, `bind_attr`, `style_var`,
`bind_each(Signal<List<T>>)`, `when(Signal<bool>)`, and the
`impl Signal<str> with AttrValue` — although none of the read-only ones
ever writes its argument. A user type implementing `Source<T>` (kolt's
StorageSignal, a RemoteSource, any A32 custom) cannot feed them. The
audit: sweep std (ui first, then everywhere) for concrete-Signal
parameters, classify READ-ONLY vs WRITE-BACK, widen the read-only ones to
`Source` bounds (`bind_attr<V: Source<str>>(...)` or the house generic
spelling), and leave the write-back pair (`bind_value`, `bind_draft`)
concrete until A32's write-side ruling. Care: bounded-generic widening
changes dispatch shape — the emission must stay byte-identical for
existing `Signal` callers (the green negative), and `process/ui.vl`'s
mirror surface moves in lockstep.
