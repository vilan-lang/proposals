# M678 — a store mirror's holds are a list, scanned and deep-copied on every hold and release, so mounting or unmounting a view costs (its holds) × (all the mirror's holds): 550 ms to unmount a 152-row list in kolt

- status: OPEN — filed 2026-10-24 (kolt: switching off the sidebar's Tasks tab)
- kind: performance (std `rpc/mirror.vl`; M)
- area: std — rpc / reactive store

`StoreMint` keeps its holds in `holds: Shared<List<MintHold>>`, and `MirrorView` keeps its client slots in `slots: Shared<List<ClientSlot>>`. Every hold and every release walks the whole list:

- `StoreMint::release(id)` walks `self.holds.read()` and rebuilds `kept` without the one hold (`kept.push(held)` for each other one), then reassigns it;
- `MirrorView::hold` / `release` / `release_base` walk `self.slots.read()` to find one slot.

`Shared::read()` answers a copy, so each walk first deep-copies the list, including every `MirrorBoundary` and its path. Building `kept` copies each surviving hold again (the emitted JS: `for (const held of __clone(self[5].v)) … kept.push(__clone(held))`). A view's cells each hold once, so unmounting a view with h holds, under a mirror holding H in all, costs about h × H deep copies.

H is everything any view holds on that store, not just the view going away. In kolt, one org store backs the sidebar's task list, the table and the board.

Measured in kolt (v0.52.0, Chrome, CPU profile of one click):

| what | tasks | other views on the store | click → next frame | in `release` + its `__clone` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sidebar Tasks tab → Messages (unmounts the list) | 16 | none | 13 ms | ~4 ms |
| same | 152 | none | 88 ms | 74 ms |
| same | 152 | task table open beside it | **550 ms** | **489 ms** |
| Messages → Tasks tab (mounts the list) | 152 | none | 61 ms | hold's walks ~25 ms |

Recommendation: key both structures by what they're looked up by. Holds go in a map from id to `MintHold`, plus a count per boundary identity for `holding`; slots go in a map from boundary identity to slot, and from slot number to slot. Look up and remove without copying the collection: iterate a borrowed view, or mutate in place under `write()`. The cloning `read()` in these hot paths is the larger constant. The list scan is what makes it quadratic. Either alone helps; both make hold and release O(1).
