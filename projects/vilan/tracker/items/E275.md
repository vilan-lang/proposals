# E275 — a written or derived `Debug` impl decides how `dbg` prints that type, and `[derive(Debug)]` spells enum variants qualified (`Shape::Circle(1.5)`), as the printer does

- status: OPEN — filed 2026-10-05 (RULED by the owner on debug-47's report; Order 48 or a follow-up lane)
- kind: tooling (M)
- area: Editor & tooling

debug-47 built `dbg`'s generated printers and S4's `Debug` impls separately: a derived `Debug` prints `Circle(1.5)`, `dbg` prints `Shape::Circle(1.5)`. RULED 2026-10-05, as recommended: (1) a written `Debug` impl overrides how `dbg` prints that type; (2) the derive changes its spelling to the qualified form so the two agree; (3) `.debug()` stays opt-in through the derive, with no implicit bound on every type (`dbg` is the no-derive path). Both backends byte-identical; the committed `native/dbg_printer.stderr` moves with (2).
