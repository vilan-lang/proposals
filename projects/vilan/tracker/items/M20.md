# M20 — a chunk snapshots its dependencies by value, so a chunk evaluated before its dependency binds `undefined` forever

- status: OPEN — m18-paper's probe P3 (2026-09-01); latent, held safe only by the route partition
- kind: bug
- area: Performance & footprint
- source: proposal/bundle-boundaries.md D5 / Q7

The emitted chunk prologue reads its dependencies once at evaluation
(`const panel = __vilan_chunks.fn.panel;`); a chunk that evaluates
before the chunk providing `panel` binds `undefined` PERMANENTLY and
throws `TypeError` even after the provider registers. Today the route
partition guarantees no chunk references another chunk's private
function, so nothing hits it — but it is the wall any nested or
shared boundary (M18) runs into first, and it is what stops route
splitting from ever extracting a shared chunk. Fix: emit chunk→chunk
references as call-site property reads (the eager forwarder already
does exactly this), keeping the by-value snapshot for eager names
only. Pin P3's shape: two chunks, evaluated in the wrong order, the
late binding resolves. Independently valuable ahead of the attribute
(the paper's Q7).

## 2026-09-01 — M18's Q7 answered: M20 goes FIRST, ahead of any attribute.
