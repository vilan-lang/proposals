# E136 — a multi-value element attribute declines the WHOLE element; its curated diagnostic is discarded and the span lands on the tag

- status: OPEN — the owner's find while migrating kolt (2026-09-04); reproduced at 289e2a2b by the triage lane
- kind: bug
- area: Language server & tooling

`<div raw("inert", has_scrim) .styled(app_shell_style)>` → one error,
`expected ';' to end this statement` at the head's closing `>`, nothing
on the comma. `let v = <div name(a, b)></div>;` → `found '/' expected an
expression`. The SELF-CLOSING `<div name(a, b)/>` gives the right
message at the comma: `found ',' expected ')' (an attribute takes one
value; a chain link starts with '.')`. (Valid spelling: `raw` is a
`Style`/`Declarations` method, not a View attribute; a two-argument
attribute has no head form — the chain links are `.attr("inert", v)` /
`.bind_attr(..)`, ui.vl ~169/~248.)

Cause: the comma arm of `parse_element_head_item` (parsing.rs ~3404)
calls `note_expected` then `return None` — a HARD decline — unlike the
`.`-chain arm (~3358) which reports and returns `Some(None)`, keeping
the element. The hard decline fails the element; `parse_atom` (~2706)
falls back to `recover_delimited("element", '<', '>')`, which re-emits
the comma failure — but it survives only if the enclosing statement
then parses; with a paired `</div>` the `<`/`>` read as comparisons,
`attempt`'s `errors.truncate(error_count)` (~962) discards it, and the
span degenerates to the operator soup.

Fix (S, low risk): mirror the chain arm — report at the comma, skip to
the matching `)`, `return Some(None)`; the element survives, the span
is the comma, nothing truncates. Pin: a paired-tag fixture beside
`"<div name(a, b)/>"` in parse_expr_regression/fixtures.rs ~522.
Related: E135 (the pair the owner hit together), E69.
