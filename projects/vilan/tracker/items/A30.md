# A30 — `ReactiveClient` disposes itself at terminal `Closed`

- status: OPEN — RULED 2026-08-29 (the owner chose the wiring over the
  caller-less seam alone: "the safer option"); build-ready
- kind: feature
- area: Reactive core & UI
- source: Order 19 S1's caller-less `dispose` + the owner's ruling, 2026-08-29

S1 gave `ReactiveClient` a `dispose()` (clear the transport handler, empty
the routes) with no caller in std — the seam for tests, HMR teardown and
fresh-client reconnects. The ruling wires it: when a connection reaches the
TERMINAL `Closed` — A26's two paths, the contract drift and the refused
`__attach` — the client disposes itself. The mirrors on that socket are
already dead (A26's own argument: the honest report), so auto-dispose
completes the honesty and frees the graph instead of leaving a wedged
client holding it. The public `dispose()` stays for the app-owned cases.
Pins ride A26's failure-mode-as-spawn-arg harness: after a drift and after
a refused re-attach, the routes are empty and the transport handler is
cleared; the SCC gate's post-disposal contract covers the graph half. A
non-terminal drop (the redial path) must NOT dispose — the negative.
