# E118 — the formatter lays out an element passed inline to a closure argument oddly

- status: OPEN — owner report 2026-09-01
- kind: bug
- area: LSP & tooling
- source: the owner's exhibit:

    overlays.attach(submenu, || <div .styled(example_padded_style)>
        <button>"Sub item"</button>
    </div>);

The element's opening tag starts mid-line (after `|| `), its children
indent one level from the STATEMENT, and the closing tag sits at the
statement's column — three anchors that don't agree. The formatter
should pick one: either break before the element (`|| ` then the
element on its own line, children +1, close aligned to the open) or
keep the inline open and align children/close to the open tag's
column. The former matches how a block body formats; recommend it.
Pin the exhibit verbatim plus the one-child and nested cases.
