The greedy-peel reading has one real hole: a closure-typed return with its OWN clause — `fun f(): (|| void) context c` — cannot say whether `c` is the function's requirement or the returned closure's clause (the peel takes it for the function); the before-the-colon form spells both (`fun f() context c: (|| void) context d`).
