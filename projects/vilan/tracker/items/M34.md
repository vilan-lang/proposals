# M34 — `vilan check` of an entry runs the whole transformer (16% of a small entry's instructions) for its one diagnostic

- status: OPEN — kolt-benchmark's find (2026-09-04; callgrind at 289e2a2b); decision needed before a slice
- kind: performance
- area: CLI & build

`compile_unit` is shared by `run`, `check`, and the watch loops, and
for an ENTRY it calls `transformer::transform` regardless of goal —
**16.1% of the lucide-stubbed client check's 3.21e9 Ir**
(`Transformer::assemble` 14.9%, `allocate_scope` 8.7%). E113 already
skips it for a module addressed by path (`!goal.emits()`, "analysis
is the whole job"), and its comment names emission's one diagnostic
— the missing `main`. Consequence: `vilan check .` on kolt costs the
same CPU as `vilan build .` (2.26 s vs 2.22 s measured 2026-09-04),
and `check` is what `--watch` and the perf harness's end-to-end rows
spawn. Decide: either `check` on an entry skips the transformer and
the transformer's `Err` conditions are enumerated and asked
analysis-side (missing `main`, and whatever else `transform` can
refuse today — list them first), or the transformer grows a
diagnose-only prefix that stops before `assemble`. Pin: `check`'s Ir
< `build`'s by the transform share on kolt, and every diagnostic
`transform` can raise still surfaces from `check` (one pin per
condition, per the per-case rule).
