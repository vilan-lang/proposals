- 2026-09-25 (the owner's rulings after Order 41's seal): RULED (a) as recommended — a second optional crate `vilan-rt-crypto` (OS randomness via `getrandom`, SHA-512 + PBKDF2), linked only when reached, `vilan-rt` itself stays dependency-free and `forbid(unsafe_code)`. The owner asked where the unsafe comes from: OS randomness is a SYSCALL — `getrandom(2)` on Linux, `BCryptGenRandom` on Windows, `SecRandomCopyBytes` on macOS — reached only through FFI (`extern "C"` calls are `unsafe` in Rust) or a crate that wraps that FFI; Rust's std exposes no stable safe random source; the one safe route is reading `/dev/urandom` through `std::fs`, which is unix-only; SHA-512 and PBKDF2 themselves are pure computation with no unsafe (SHA-1/SHA-256 are already hand-written in vilan-rt).
