### homebrew-vilan: by workflow or by hand?
Automated by `release.yml` job `publish-brew` (reads `scripts/brew-formula.sh`, pushes `Formula/vilan.rb` to the tap), **but it has been silently doing nothing since 2026-09-28**:
- The live tap formula at `Formula/vilan.rb` says `version "0.41.1"`. Tap commits: 2026-09-27 "vilan 0.41.1", 2026-09-26 "0.41.0", 2026-09-01 "0.40.0", 2026-08-29 "0.39.0". Nothing since.
- Commit `1946f746` (2026-09-28) "move publish-brew's tap-token mint off actions/create-github-app-token's deprecated app-id" changed the workflow to read secrets `TAP_APP_CLIENT_ID` + `TAP_APP_PRIVATE_KEY`. The repo's actual secrets (`gh secret list`) are `TAP_APP_ID` (2026-07-29) and `TAP_APP_PRIVATE_KEY`; **`TAP_APP_CLIENT_ID` does not exist**. The job's guard step then emits a notice ("TAP_APP_CLIENT_ID / TAP_APP_PRIVATE_KEY are not configured ... still points at the previous release") and skips "Mint the tap token / checksums / Update the tap". Verified on the v0.56.1 release run 43984019501: `publish-brew` conclusion success, with exactly those 3 steps skipped. The job reports green, so nothing alerts.
- The website landing page (`src/page.vl:233`) and `vilan/docs/tour/hello-vilan.md:32` advertise `brew install vilan-lang/vilan/vilan`, which installs 0.41.1.
- The vilan repo's own `homebrew/Formula/vilan.rb` is deliberately a pinned seed fixture at 0.14.0 (documented; byte-compared by `brew_formula.rs`). Not a defect.
